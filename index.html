💡 Team Recommendation: Swap Slides 6 & 7 in Your Template

The original deck has Virtual Sessions as Slide 6 and Import Timeline as Slide 7. I swapped them in my template — and I'd recommend everyone does the same. Here's why:

❌ Original Order

Slide 5: What to Expect
Slide 6: Virtual Sessions
Slide 7: Import Timeline

Problem: You're scheduling training before you've discussed the menu or data. You don't know the build complexity yet, so you can't frame what training should focus on.

✅ Updated Order (Recommended)

Slide 5: What to Expect
Slide 6: Import Timeline ✱
Slide 7: Virtual Sessions ✱

Better: You discuss menu/data first, understand the complexity and scope, then schedule training knowing exactly what to prioritize. The conversation flows naturally: setup → data → schedule.

By the time you get to scheduling, you already know the seller's menu complexity, data needs, and priorities — so you can say "Based on what we just discussed, I'd recommend we focus our training on X and Y" instead of scheduling blind. All the slide cues below follow this updated order.

⚠️ What’s NOT Bundled (Even on Premium)

Even on Premium, some Square products remain separate subscriptions or costs:

💰 Square Payroll

Still a separate paid add-on.

🏢 Franchise Suite

Square for Franchises — separate add-on.

📺 KDS & Kiosk Apps

Per-device software subscriptions, managed under their own entries in Pricing & Subscriptions.

🌐 Domains & Text Overages

Custom domains renew annually with separate charges. Marketing/loyalty text messages beyond the plan’s monthly allotment incur per-text fees.

💻 Hardware

Square Register, Terminal, Handheld, KDS screens, printers, kiosks, etc. are always sold separately from software plans.

🔎 Quick check: When a seller asks “Is X included in my plan?”, first check whether it’s a Square One bundled feature or one of these explicit add-ons.

🔍 How to Confirm a Seller’s Plan & Features

Because pricing and feature allocation can change, always confirm against live account data and the Help Center.

In Square Dashboard (seller-specific)

  1. Sign into the seller’s Square Dashboard
  2. Navigate to: Settings → Account & Settings → Pricing & subscriptions
  3. From there you can:
    • See which plan (Free, Plus, Premium) is active
    • Review which add-ons are enabled (Payroll, KDS, Kiosk, etc.)
    • Start or change free trials where available
